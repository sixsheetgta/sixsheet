About SixSheet

SixSheet is a free, spoiler-free GTA VI cheat sheet: launch prep, tested cheat codes, money methods, collectible trackers and new finds.

How entries get checked

  • Every code, money method and find links to where it came from, and credits the player who found it.
  • Something is marked tested only with clear in-game video or several independent confirmations.
  • No story spoilers, leaks, rumors or piracy. Secrets that might spoil a surprise stay hidden until you tap them.
