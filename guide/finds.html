Latest GTA VI finds

Every shop is open 24/7, even the barber
All of the game's 700-plus stores stay open around the clock, so a haircut, manicure, coffee or new hat is available at any hour as long as you have the cash. Rockstar's player-systems design director told Kotaku they chose convenience over realism because arriving at a closed shop got frustrating, and it means any store can be robbed at any time.
Source: Kotaku (Rebekah Valentine)
Fully playable arcade games on in-game consoles
Rockstar built its own set of video games you can play inside GTA VI, including a zombie shooter, a top-down sci-fi shooter and a bar-style hunting light-gun game. They come with menus and multiple stages, so they feel like real games rather than quick mini-games.
Source: Kotaku (interview with Rockstar's Rob Nelson); clip shared by @TheGameVerse via Digital Foundry
Flip cars back over and sit down anywhere
Walk up to an overturned car and click the left stick to brace against it, then hold the action button to roll it back onto its wheels (heavy cars or low stamina can make it fail). The same push works on boats and jet skis on the sand. Holding the crouch button while on foot makes your character sit or lean against a wall.
Source: Kotaku (Zack Zwiezen)
Spoiler-ish. Tap to revealA nod to the famous 'giant enemy crab' meme
An abandoned research site in the open world hides a jump-scare tribute to the giant enemy crab meme from E3 2006. It's a random discovery while exploring, not part of a mission.
Source: Polygon (clip by YouTuber xGarbett)
Spoiler-ish. Tap to revealA famous TV show's Miami apartment
Vice City includes a close copy of the apartment complex from the Dexter TV series, with a look-alike of his fishing boat tied up at a nearby dock. You can visit the outside walkway, but the door stays locked.
Source: GameSpot (first spotted by @Saukko505 on X)
Snapmatic jokes about waiting for Race and Chase 7
Scroll the in-game Snapmatic feed early on and one user gripes that a Barnyard Blue cartoon game came out before Race and Chase 7. It's Rockstar's own spin on the "we got X before GTA 6" meme, since Race'n'Chase was the first GTA's working title.
Source: Kotaku (spotted by players on X, e.g. @SoggyNeko)
Spoiler-ish. Tap to revealGlowing meteorite chunks to collect
Meteorite chunks are a collectible, and they give off a glow after dark, so night is the easiest time to hunt for them. First shown on video by @xGarbett; The Big Lead's collectibles guide lists several.
Source: @xGarbett on X, also in The Big Lead's collectibles guide (thebiglead.com)
Cash with Red Dead presidents on it
Look closely at the in-game bank notes: the faces on the $10 and $1 bills reuse the portraits of two presidents from Red Dead Redemption 2's cigarette cards. Spotted by Reddit user u/cph2001 in Rockstar's official screenshots.
Source: u/cph2001 on r/GTA6, reported by Dexerto and RockstarINTEL
You can pet cats and dogs
Walk up to a friendly cat or dog and you can give it a pet. Players started sharing clips of it within hours of the first unlocks. Careful with car doors nearby.
Source: Clips from nyrhae and others, rounded up by Kotaku (Kenneth Shepard)
Spoiler-ish. Tap to revealA pet snake you can feed
Some homes keep a pet snake in a glass tank, and you can drop in a mouse and watch it eat. The feeding animation is surprisingly lifelike.
Source: NikTek on X and an xQc stream clip, rounded up by Kotaku
Collectible trading cards: 18 sets, 480 cards
Trading cards are hidden all over the map, on shelves, walls, phone boxes and tool boxes. There are 18 sets covering fictional sports teams, comics and more. Once selling unlocks, pawn shops buy them for roughly $20 up to $1,000+ depending on rarity, and selling a card doesn't remove it from your collection.
Source: PowerPyx collectible guide
Spoiler-ish. Tap to revealA goggle-wearing dog that drives a jet ski
Swim far enough out and you may find a dog in goggles sitting on a jet ski. Hop on and the dog keeps its paws on the handlebars while you ride along.
Source: ChizPlays on X, covered by Kotaku
Spoiler-ish. Tap to revealA familiar boat from GTA V
Michael's well-known yacht from GTA V shows up in Leonida, and its GPS screen shows a slice of the old Los Santos map. A fun nod for longtime fans.
Source: riff_gta on X; also listed by Sportskeeda
Spoiler-ish. Tap to revealA funny Easter egg waits at the top of a tall tower
A long climb ends at a nest of painted eggs, and throwing one off the top gets a comic reaction from someone down below. The climb takes about seven minutes. Confirmed on video by several players, including xGarbett, elrubius and Jynxzi.
Source: xGarbett on X, covered by GameSpot (Eddie Makuch); also shown on stream by elrubius and Jynxzi
Tiny animation details in the Guardian's hands-on preview
Jason can take bites of a donut in one hand while sipping a beer with the other. When he breaks into a run with a six-pack, he hikes it into the crook of his arm and the drinks slosh inside the bottles. Seen in a Rockstar-led demo, not yet checked by players.
Source: The Guardian (Keza MacDonald)
