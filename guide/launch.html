Checklist

  • Free up 212 GB. Xbox Series S 512 GB owners: expect to delete a couple of big games.
  • Finish the preload. Digital pre-load has been open since November 10. Done when the game tile shows a countdown instead of a Play button. If you have a code-in-box copy, redeem it as soon as it arrives.
  • Buy before Nov 20 for the Vice City Pack. Both editions include it. Digital copies also include one month of GTA+; claim it on the GTA+ page, and cancel if you do not want it to continue as a paid plan.
  • Check your account region's unlock time. Your platform account region sets the unlock. Changing the console clock does nothing.
  • Mute spoiler words on X, Reddit and YouTube. Earlier time zones unlock up to 19 hours before the Americas. Streams of the opening will be everywhere.
  • Keep the console on and updated. Two updates have rolled out so far: 1.002 from November 13 and 1.002.001 from November 16, and more may follow. Leave the console on, or in Rest or Sleep mode with auto-updates on, and check for updates again on November 18. PS5 owners: system software 26.07-14.23.00 (security fixes) is also out, so install it before unlock (playstation.com/support).

Confirmed facts

Release
Thursday, November 19, 2026
Source: Rockstar Games
Unlock time
Rockstar Support confirms midnight local time on Nov 19 in every region, on both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. New Zealand goes first; US store listings show midnight Eastern (05:00 UTC).
Source: Rockstar Support
Platforms
PS5, Xbox Series X and Series S. No PC version announced.
Source: Rockstar Support
Xbox Cloud Gaming
Playable day one via Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox One and Series consoles, starting at 1 a.m. ET on November 19. Streams at Series S settings; the game is sold separately.
Source: Xbox Wire
Performance
Rockstar says the game targets 30 fps on every console. PS5 Pro adds PSSR upscaling for a sharper image plus ray-traced shadows that only the Pro gets. Remote Play works on both PlayStation and Xbox.
Source: Rockstar Newswire
Price
Standard $79.99 / Ultimate $99.99 (Ultimate Upgrade $20 on the US Xbox Store)
Source: Xbox Store
Physical copies
Code in a box. No disc at launch. Rockstar says boxed copies are available starting November 12 so you can redeem and pre-load. There is no physical Ultimate Edition.
Source: Rockstar Support
Download size
Up to 212 GB, depending on console
Source: Rockstar via GamesRadar+
Pre-load
Digital pre-load started November 10: midnight local time on PS5, and 12 a.m. GMT worldwide on Xbox. Redeem a code-in-box copy as soon as you have it (retail availability from November 12).
Source: Rockstar Support
First update
Version 1.002 started rolling out in stages on November 13, about 1 GB on PS5. Rockstar hasn't posted patch notes. Let it install before launch night.
Source: Polygon
Second update
Version 1.002.001 began rolling out November 16: about 600 MB on PS5 and about 1.6 GB on Xbox Series X|S. PS5 gets it in stages, so check again if it hasn't shown up yet. Rockstar hasn't posted patch notes.
Source: VICE
Early-purchase bonus
Vintage Vice City Pack: a '55 Vapid Stanier sedan and garage, outfits and hairstyles, and an exclusive weapon pattern. Included with purchases before Nov 20. Digital editions also include one month of GTA+.
Source: Rockstar Support
Free map and poster
From Nov 19, Rockstar's official GTA VI site will offer a free printable cover-art poster and Leonida map. A double-sided printed version follows on the Rockstar Store shortly after launch for $0.99, or free with any store order.
Source: Rockstar via Polygon
Mode at launch
Single-player
Source: PlayStation
