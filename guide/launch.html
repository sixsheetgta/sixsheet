Checklist

  1. Free up 212 GB. Xbox Series S 512 GB owners: expect to delete a couple of big games.
  2. Finish the preload. Done when the game tile shows a countdown instead of a Play button.
  3. Buy before Nov 20 for the Vintage Vice City Pack. Both editions include it.
  4. Check your account region's unlock time. Your PSN account region sets the unlock. Changing the console clock does nothing.
  5. Mute spoiler words on X, Reddit and YouTube. Earlier time zones unlock up to 19 hours before the Americas. Streams of the opening will be everywhere.
  6. Leave the console on and updated on launch night. A day-one patch is likely. Let it download before midnight.
