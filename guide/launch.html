Checklist

  1. Free up 212 GB. Xbox Series S 512 GB owners: expect to delete a couple of big games.
  2. Finish the preload. Digital pre-load has been open since November 10. Done when the game tile shows a countdown instead of a Play button. If you have a code-in-box copy, redeem it as soon as it arrives.
  3. Buy before Nov 20 for the Vintage Vice City Pack. Both editions include it. Digital copies also include one month of GTA+; claim it on the GTA+ page, and cancel if you do not want it to continue as a paid plan.
  4. Check your account region's unlock time. Your platform account region sets the unlock. Changing the console clock does nothing.
  5. Mute spoiler words on X, Reddit and YouTube. Earlier time zones unlock up to 19 hours before the Americas. Streams of the opening will be everywhere.
  6. Leave the console on and updated on launch night. A day-one patch is likely. Leave the console on, or in Rest or Sleep mode with auto-updates on, so any update can install before you play.

Confirmed facts

Release
Thursday, November 19, 2026
Source: Rockstar Games
Unlock time
PlayStation has confirmed a rolling midnight-local launch on Nov 19, starting at midnight NZDT. The US Xbox Store lists midnight Eastern (05:00 UTC).
Source: PlayStation Blog
Platforms
PS5, Xbox Series X and Series S. No PC version announced.
Source: Rockstar Support
Xbox Cloud Gaming
Playable day one via Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox One and Series consoles, starting at 1 a.m. ET on November 19. Streams at Series S settings; the game is sold separately.
Source: Xbox Wire
Price
Standard $79.99 / Ultimate $99.99 (Ultimate Upgrade $20 on the US Xbox Store)
Source: Xbox Store
Physical copies
Code in a box. No disc at launch. Rockstar says boxed copies are available starting November 12 so you can redeem and pre-load. There is no physical Ultimate Edition.
Source: Rockstar Support
Download size
Up to 212 GB, depending on console
Source: Rockstar via GamesRadar+
Pre-load
Digital pre-load started November 10: midnight local time on PS5, and 12 a.m. GMT worldwide on Xbox. Redeem a code-in-box copy as soon as you have it (retail availability from November 12).
Source: Rockstar Support
Early-purchase bonus
Vintage Vice City Pack: a '55 Vapid Stanier sedan and garage, outfits and hairstyles, and an exclusive weapon pattern. Included with purchases before Nov 20. Digital editions also include one month of GTA+.
Source: Rockstar Support
Mode at launch
Single-player
Source: PlayStation
