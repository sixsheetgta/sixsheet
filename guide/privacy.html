Privacy

Last updated 2026-11-17. Short version: SixSheet has no accounts and doesn't collect your personal information.

What stays on your device

Your checklist and tracker progress, spoiler setting, console choice and similar preferences are saved in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device, and clearing your browser data deletes them.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses to run and secure the service. See GitHub's privacy statement.

Ads

SixSheet shows ads from Google AdSense to pay for the site. Google and its partners use cookies to show ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can turn off personalized ads at Google Ad Settings, and read how Google uses this data at policies.google.com. Visitors in the EU, UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.
